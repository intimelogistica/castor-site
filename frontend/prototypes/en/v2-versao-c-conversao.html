Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost? How long does it take? Everything you want to know.

Direct answers about the budget for a wood house — no fine print.

How much does a wood house cost?

The value depends on the model, size, finish and delivery location — so there is no fixed price per m² that suits all projects.

The most accurate way to find out how much the wood house you want costs is to request a quote: fill out the form and receive prices and a deadline without obligation.

What is the deadline to build my house?

The deadline combines production, transportation and assembly, and varies depending on the project and the location of the work. In your quote you receive the estimated deadline for your specific case.

Do you have financing or installments?

Yes, check payment terms and financing options via Whats App. Our team guides you on the path that best fits your plan.

Do I need to have the land ready?

Yes — the house is built on your land. In the initial conversation, we evaluate the site's accessibility and infrastructure to guide you on what to prepare before assembly.

What wood is used in construction?

We work with reforestation wood and selected hardwood, with appropriate treatment for structural use and durability.

Does the house have a guarantee?

Yes. All Castor homes are delivered with a structural guarantee — coverage details are provided in the quote and contract.

Can I customize or change the design?

It can — and it is our standard. Projects are initial suggestions; We create a new project according to the needs of each client. Tell us what you need and we’ll design it together.
